Woven
Deep dive · Codex to receipt

What actually happens.

One request in ChatGPT or Codex, traced from the model’s tool call to the pickup receipt.

01 You → host

Ask in the chat.

“Charging kit for MacBook Air, iPhone, AirPods — under S$150, pickup today.”
02 model → Woven

The model picks start_mission.

Codex extracts budget, devices, destination, and pickup into one MCP call.
03 cart engine

Filter → assemble → rank.

Incompatible or out-of-stock offers drop; complete one-merchant carts are ranked and explained.
04 Woven → widget

The app renders in-chat.

The model sees only a text summary; the widget gets the full view over the MCP Apps bridge.
05 You → widget

Explore with app-only tools.

select_cart runs from your clicks — the model cannot call it.
06 widget → guard

Review mints a mandate.

create_checkout_preview rechecks price and stock, binds the hash, and hides a one-time nonce from the model.
07 You → guard

One click confirms the exact terms.

confirm_purchase must present the nonce, the mandate hash, and an idempotency key.
08 adapter → receipt

Authorize, commit, hand over.

The payment adapter approves, declines, or reverses; stock and order commit atomically; the receipt lands in the chat.

Why it can’t go wrong quietly

Price and stock recheck twice · mandates expire · duplicate confirms are idempotent · a decline creates no order · merchant failure after authorization reverses.
